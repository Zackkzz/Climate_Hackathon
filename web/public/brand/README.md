# Meterwise brand files

- `meterwise-mark.svg`: the mark (roof line over a meter dial) in navy, for light backgrounds.
- `meterwise-mark-reversed.svg`: the mark in white, for navy backgrounds.
- `meterwise-logo.svg` and `meterwise-logo-reversed.svg`: mark plus wordmark (Public Sans Bold).
- `meterwise-favicon.svg`: white mark on a navy square, legible at 16 px.

Navy is #0B2A4A. Keep clear space of half the mark's height around the logo. Do not recolour, stretch or add effects.
